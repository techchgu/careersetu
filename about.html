About CareerSetu AI

Your Career, Guided by AI

CareerSetu AI is a student-focused career guidance platform designed to help students understand their skills, explore opportunities and build a clear path toward their career goals.

About CareerSetu AI

Choosing a career can be difficult when students have many options but don't know which direction matches their interests, skills and goals.

CareerSetu AI brings important career preparation activities together in one student-focused platform.

The platform uses AI-assisted analysis to help students understand their profile, identify skill gaps, create a roadmap and prepare for career opportunities.

What CareerSetu AI Focuses On
